Two ways to use this: One-time sort works exactly like a normal upload-and-go tool — nothing is saved anywhere, close the tab and it's gone (print or export first). Recipe database instead saves every dish's ingredients and per-pax quantities in this browser, reloading automatically every time you reopen this file — no re-uploading week to week, just tick dishes, enter pax and a date, and Generate. That database is stored via this browser's local storage, tied to this device/browser: export a backup occasionally (button above, in Recipe database mode) in case you switch computers, switch browsers, or clear browsing data. Sorting is keyword-based and won't be perfect — use the section dropdown on any row to move it. Prep stage is auto-assigned on a 4-day lead time (Day 1 collect/trim/wash/count, Day 2 cook/steam/roast, Day 3 portion/tray up/box off, Day 4 box-off continuation/production tools) based on each task's wording, then mapped onto real weekdays — in Recipe database mode, working backward from the event date you enter each week (a Saturday event runs Wed/Thu/Fri/Sat prep); in One-time sort mode, from a weekday named in the dish's tab (e.g. "Mains Beef Fillet Saturday") or set manually per dish. The "By prep day" view groups every dish's tasks by that real weekday across the whole week. Team leader names, section moves, pax counts, and day assignments are saved automatically to this browser as you work, so a refresh or an accidental close won't lose them — this week's pax/date selections in Recipe database mode are the one exception and reset each week by design. Export or print if you want a copy outside this browser.

Getting started

A quick walkthrough of the whole workflow, from raw files to a printed pack for the kitchen. Each step below matches a button or screen in the tool — work through them top to bottom the first few times, then use this page as a reference.

Preplist tab
01

Load a preplist

Drop an .xlsx preplist, or one or more production tool PDFs, into the dropzone. Every tab in a workbook is read as one dish; a PDF is read from each dish's front page, so its quantities are finished/cooked weights (check the production tool for raw weights).

Choose "recipe database" mode if you want to build a reusable library you'll draw on every week, or "one-time sort" for a single preplist you won't reuse. Either way, your work saves automatically to this browser as you go.

02

Method, captured as a real image — no parsing to get wrong

The printed sheet — by dish, by section, or by prep day — ends with a consolidated page showing just the Method section cropped straight from each dish's and sub-recipe's own production tool page, from "Method:" down through the Batch number row. It's the real pixels, not re-typed text, packed together so a dish with several sub-recipes doesn't need a separate page per one.

A "📖 View original" button also appears next to each dish on screen, for paging through its full production tool pages (not just the Method crop) with Previous/Next and a Print button any time.

Both of these are PDF-only — a spreadsheet has no production tool page to capture from.

03

Review the sorted categories

The tool splits every task into Vegetables, Proteins, Sauces/Purees/Gels, Garnishes, Big Jobs, and Box off — assign a team leader to each and switch to the "By prep day" view to see tasks grouped by real weekday.

04

Print or export

Section moves and team leader names save automatically to this browser as you go — a refresh or accidental close won't lose them. Print or export when you're ready for a copy outside the browser.

05

Export to Excel — an editable, re-uploadable copy

Each view has its own "📊 Export to Excel" button, laid out to match how you're looking at it — By dish gives one sheet per dish, By team section one sheet per section, By prep day one sheet per day (several dishes can share a sheet when grouped this way). Open it in Excel or Google Sheets to update quantities, sections or prep days as the function changes, then upload that same file back into the tool later and it reads straight back in.

By dish uses the original tab names: the workbook's own tab names in the same order, or for a PDF each recipe's title as printed. By team section and By prep day name each tab after the section or day. Every export has a Source tab column with the exact original name of the tab (or PDF recipe) each row came from. Names are made Excel-safe: at most 31 characters, no [ ] : * ? / \, and no two tabs the same; any name Excel forces us to change is listed in the message after the export. For the By-dish export specifically, a dish name longer than that comes back under its truncated tab name on re-upload, rather than its full original title — the By-section and By-day exports don't have this limit, since several dishes sharing one tab always keep their full names.

Kitchen tools (new in 3.3)
06

Try it with the sample menu

No file handy? Press “Try with sample menu” under the dropzone. A made-up five-dish menu loads with days, pax, sections, allergens, suppliers and prices, so every view, print and export can be tried in seconds.

It is clearly marked SAMPLE DATA on screen and in prints. “Clear sample” removes it, and any sample suppliers or prices, in one click.

07

Allergen tags

Open “4 · Ingredients” under the list. Each ingredient gets suggested tags from its name (gluten, dairy, egg, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, soy, fish, crustacea, molluscs, lupin), shown with a ? until you confirm or edit them. Add your own tags (e.g. celery, sulphites) and dish-level tags. Confirmed tags are remembered on this device and saved with dishes in the recipe database.

Tags show in every view, every printout and an Allergens column in all three Excel exports.

Suggestions come from a keyword list and can be wrong or incomplete. Always check them against supplier specifications. Prepline does not guarantee allergen information.

08

Order sheet by supplier

Give each ingredient a supplier in the Ingredients panel (remembered for next time), then press “Order sheet (by supplier)”. Totals from every ticked dish are added up per ingredient, rolled up to kg or L and rounded up to a sensible order quantity. Items with no supplier are listed last.

Print one supplier’s sheet or all of them, or export to Excel with an editable Order qty column.

09

Cost per dish

Enter each ingredient’s price once, per kg, L or each. Prepline shows the cost of each dish, the cost per pax and the total for the ticked dishes, and flags missing prices or units that don’t fit (e.g. a price per kg for an item counted in bunches).

The Excel exports get Price and Cost columns. Cost, dish cost and cost per pax are formulas, so they recalculate when you change pax in Excel.

Prices are used as you enter them (ex or inc GST is your choice). Missing prices count as zero, so a total with missing prices is a minimum.

10

Share a section to phones

In the By team section view, press “📱 Share to phone” on a section. A QR code and a link open that section’s list read-only on any phone, with no app, account or server: the list travels inside the link itself.

One QR code holds a link of up to 2,953 characters: about 200 typical tasks, though under about 60 tasks the code is much easier to scan. Bigger lists are split into parts (Part 1 of 2 …); a very big list offers the link only, to send by email, Teams or text.

The shared list is a snapshot. Share again after changes.

11

Install Prepline on a phone or computer

On the website, Prepline can be installed like an app: “Install” in Chrome or Edge, or Share → “Add to Home Screen” on an iPhone. Once opened, it works offline.

When a new version is published, a bar offers “Reload” to switch to it.

12

Feedback

The Feedback button (top right) builds a short message and copies it, to paste into an email, Teams or a text to whoever shared Prepline with you. It includes the app version and browser only; your list is added only if you tick “Include my current list”.

Help shape Prepline – 1-min survey (next to Feedback) opens a short anonymous survey in a new tab. Nothing is sent unless you open it and press Send.

Email us (next to Feedback) or Email feedback in the Feedback window opens your own email app with a message to Prepline support at autoprod.help@gmail.com. A small survey QR code also sits in a corner of printouts and in the share-to-phone window; it is drawn inside the app, no outside service is used.
